What Daftar does

  • Fast entry

    Income, expense or a transfer between accounts in a few steps, in Arabic (right to left) or English.

  • Debts, customers and suppliers

    Credit invoices, part payments and a clear balance for each person.

  • Same data everywhere

    Record on the phone and see it on the site, and the other way round, from one database.

  • Works offline

    Entries are saved on your device and sent when the connection returns, without duplicates.

  • A record that is never edited

    Entries are not erased. A correction is a reversing entry that keeps the original.

  • Dark and light mode

    A comfortable screen in any light.
